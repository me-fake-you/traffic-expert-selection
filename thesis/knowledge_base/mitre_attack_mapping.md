# MITRE ATT&CK background mapping

## Command and control background

Encrypted application-layer protocols can be used for command and control, but
protocol encryption alone is not evidence of an ATT&CK technique. A mapping is
background taxonomy and should be presented only when observed detector
evidence independently supports the described behavior.

## Exfiltration background

Outbound transfer behavior may relate to exfiltration techniques when combined
with corroborating endpoint, identity, and temporal evidence. The taxonomy must
not be treated as a family attribution or as proof that exfiltration occurred.
