## A concrete prefix example

The verified table reports 4,938 requests, including 2,145 malicious requests. At eight packets, 2,743 requests are supported and 178 are malicious; at sixteen packets, 2,598 are supported and 139 are malicious. Supported-only Macro-F1 is 1.0 in these recorded configurations, but all-malicious-request recall is only 178/2,145 and 139/2,145, respectively. The unsupported requests must not disappear from the conclusion. This is a bounded local replay, not an external live deployment.

## Relationship to the small paper

The top-level `traffic_selection/` and `results/` retain the separately frozen controlled expert-selection study. A large architecture diagram documents system context; it does not mean that TLS, LLM advice, four-way fusion, or every thesis module was evaluated in that binary comparison. The broader thesis record is published for inspection, not merged into the short paper's numerical claims.
